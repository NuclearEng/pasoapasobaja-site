Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026. Last updated October 26, 2026.

This policy explains how CIS Consulting ("we", "us"), the developer and data controller (responsable) of the Paso a Paso Baja app, the business portal and this website, handles information. Contact: hometownintelligence@gmail.com. If the English and Spanish versions differ, the English version controls to the extent permitted by law.

Summary

  • We collect as little as possible. Your property information stays on your iPhone and, if you choose, in your own private iCloud account, which we can't read.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites, and we don't sell or "share" (for cross-context advertising) personal information.
  • Document scanning, translation, voice input and "Ask my documents" run on your device.
  • Community sharing is optional and off by default, and what's shared is rounded and anonymous.

1. Information on your device

Your property profile, roadmap progress, deadlines, notes, expenses and vault documents are stored on your device with iOS data protection. Vault documents and sensitive fields (such as a trust number or cadastral key) are additionally encrypted on your device.

2. Private iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync, your data is stored in your own CloudKit private database, operated by Apple and accessible only to your Apple Account. We cannot access it. Vault documents are encrypted on your device before upload, and the encryption key is kept in your iCloud Keychain. Storage counts against your iCloud quota. You can turn sync off in Settings. Apple's handling is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. On-device features

Text recognition (OCR), camera and document translation, speech recognition for voice input, and the assistant and "Ask my documents" use Apple's on-device frameworks (including Apple's on-device Foundation Models). Your documents, photos, recordings and their text are not sent to us or to any third-party AI or translation service. If iOS needs to download a language or model, that download comes from Apple.

4. Community sharing (optional, off by default)

You can choose, separately, to share what you paid (costs), how long steps took (timelines) and whether an estimate was right (outcomes). Shared items contain only rounded ranges, a town area and a month or quarter. They never include your name, contact details, address, documents, or device or account identifiers, and they are not linked to you. They are sent to the app's CloudKit public database after a random delay, and nothing is shown until at least 5 people report the same item. You can withdraw your cost reports in Settings → My contributions, and Delete all data removes pending reports. Aggregated statistics that can't identify anyone may be kept and shown after you withdraw.

5. Directory (Rolodex) reports, claims and reviews

If you report a problem with a listing, claim a listing, or answer a "Did you hire them?" yes/no review, the item is stored in the app's CloudKit public database together with an app-specific, anonymous CloudKit user record ID that Apple provides. We use it to prevent abuse and duplicates, let you see or withdraw your own submissions, and moderate. We don't receive your name or Apple ID email from this ID. Reviews are shown only in aggregate; reports are read only by moderators.

6. Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We don't receive your payment card details. The app checks your purchase status on your device, and we may receive purchase and subscription status information from Apple (not linked to your identity) to provide and support what you bought. Most of the app is free and needs no purchase or account; buying Plus or Pro doesn't send us any more information about you. App Store links and QR codes we share (including the codes businesses display) may carry a campaign name so Apple can show us total downloads per campaign. They never identify who shared, scanned or downloaded.

7. Business portal (businesses only)

Businesses that manage a listing sign in with Sign in with Apple. We process the business contact information you provide (business name, contact name, phone, email, website, address, category, service areas, profile content, and the logo and photos you upload) to publish and manage the listing, verify claims and provide Pro features. Web Pro payments are processed by Stripe, which receives your payment details under Stripe's Privacy Policy; we receive only billing status and limited details (such as the last four digits and expiry). Photos and logos are reviewed by our staff before they appear; only approved images are shown, and you can remove them at any time. Approved listing information, including your public listing page (for example /p/your-listing), the verified badge and QR code, is, by design, public.

7a. Anonymous listing view counts

When someone opens a business's public listing page or listing screen in the app, we add one to that listing's view total for the day. We store only the listing, the date (day only) and the count. We don't store who viewed it, IP addresses, device or account identifiers, cookies, the time of day or where the visitor came from, and counts can't be linked to anyone. The app sends at most one count per listing per day and includes only the listing id. Businesses see only totals.

7b. Closing day, meetings, document sharing, badges and reviews

  • Closing-day tracker (Live Activity). If you start it, your Lock Screen shows which closing checkpoint you're on. It holds only the checkpoint number and a timer. It never includes names, amounts or addresses, and nothing is sent to us.
  • Bilingual meeting mode. Speech is recognized and translated on your iPhone. The agenda checkmarks exist only while the screen is open. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent.
  • Deal red-flag check. Your answers stay in memory on your iPhone and are cleared when you close the screen. They are never saved or sent.
  • Sharing documents with an heir or co-owner (off by default). Only if you turn it on and choose documents, copies of those documents are stored in your own iCloud account and shared, read-only, with the people you invite through Apple's sharing screen. We never see who you invite or the documents. Those copies are protected by iCloud, not by the vault's additional encryption. "Stop sharing" deletes the copies.
  • Verified seal and reply badge. Our staff check public registries (SEP, SAT, Colegio de Notarios) and store only the result, the date and the source. Reply badges come from reply times businesses confirm, at least 5 in 90 days, with no buyer identity.
  • Community fee ranges and "buyers like you" timelines. Shown only when at least 5 people contributed, as rounded ranges. Single contributions can't be recovered. They are estimates.
  • Written reviews. Optional text (up to 500 characters). We remove phone numbers, emails and IDs on your device, and a moderator reviews it before it appears. Reviews aren't linked to your name.

8. Website and content updates

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the app downloads guide updates from it. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. We don't use cookies or analytics on this site, and content update requests contain no personal data.

9. Support

If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond and keep records of the request.

10. What we don't do

No advertising, no third-party analytics, no data brokers, no cross-app tracking, no sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising, and no use of sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. Crash reports come only through Apple, and only if you've chosen to share them with developers.

11. Why we use information (legal bases)

To provide the features you request (contract), to keep the service secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interests), with your consent for community sharing (which you can withdraw anytime), and to meet legal obligations.

12. Who receives information

Only service providers that run the service for us: Apple (iCloud/CloudKit, App Store, Sign in with Apple), Stripe (web payments), Cloudflare (business portal hosting) and GitHub (website hosting). Cloudflare may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the portal; we don't store them. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in a merger or sale of the business, subject to this policy.

13. Retention

Data on your device and in your private iCloud stays until you delete it. Community items are kept until you withdraw them (aggregates may remain). Reports, claims and reviews are kept while relevant to the listing and for up to 2 years after resolution for abuse prevention. Business portal data is kept while the account is active and then deleted within 90 days, except billing records we must keep for tax and legal reasons (generally up to 5 years in Mexico). Uploaded photos are kept while the listing is active (rejected or removed photos are deleted within 30 days). Daily view counts contain no personal data and may be kept as aggregates. Support emails are kept up to 2 years.

14. Security

We use on-device encryption, iOS data protection, Apple's iCloud security and access controls for moderation tools. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

15. Deleting your data

  • App: Settings → Delete all data removes your data from the device and your private iCloud database and removes pending community reports. Deleting the app removes local data.
  • Public records (your reports, claims or reviews) and business portal accounts: email us from the address on file or describe the submission, and we'll delete or anonymize it within 30 days unless we must keep it by law.

16. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, object to or limit its use, and withdraw consent. Email hometownintelligence@gmail.com. We'll verify your request in a reasonable way and respond within the time the law requires. We won't discriminate against you for using your rights. You may use an authorized agent where the law permits.

  • Mexico (LFPDPPP): you have ARCO rights (Access, Rectification, Cancellation, Opposition) and may revoke consent or limit use. Your request should include your name, a way to reply, a description of the data and the right you want to exercise, and documents proving identity. We'll answer within 20 business days and act within 15 business days after that. You may also contact the Mexican data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): in the last 12 months we collected identifiers (anonymous CloudKit user record ID; business contact data for portal users), commercial information (purchase status), and user content (reports, reviews, claims), from you and Apple, for the purposes above. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use sensitive personal information beyond what's allowed.
  • Canada (PIPEDA): you may access and correct your information and complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
  • EU/UK/EEA: the app is aimed at Baja California Sur, but if GDPR applies you have the rights listed above plus the right to complain to your local supervisory authority.
  • Washington (My Health My Data Act): we don't collect consumer health data, so this law doesn't apply to the app.

17. International transfers

We operate from the United States, and our providers process data in the United States and other countries. Your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, Mexico and Canada, with protections required by law. Under the LFPDPPP, transfers to our service providers don't require your consent.

18. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child gave us information, email us and we'll delete it.

19. Changes

We'll post updates here with a new effective date and, for material changes, tell you in the app.

Resumen

  • Recabamos lo mínimo posible. La información de tu propiedad se queda en tu iPhone y, si lo eliges, en tu propia cuenta privada de iCloud, que nosotros no podemos leer.
  • Sin anuncios, sin SDK de analítica o publicidad de terceros, sin rastreo entre apps o sitios, y no vendemos ni compartimos datos personales para publicidad.
  • El escaneo de documentos, la traducción, la entrada por voz y "Pregunta a mis documentos" funcionan en tu dispositivo.
  • Compartir con la comunidad es opcional, está desactivado por defecto y lo compartido es redondeado y anónimo.

1. Información en tu dispositivo

Tu perfil de propiedad, avance, fechas límite, notas, gastos y documentos de la bóveda se guardan en tu dispositivo con la protección de datos de iOS. Los documentos de la bóveda y los campos sensibles (como el número de fideicomiso o la clave catastral) llevan cifrado adicional en el dispositivo.

2. Sincronización privada con iCloud (opcional)

Si activas la sincronización, tus datos se guardan en tu propia base de datos privada de CloudKit, operada por Apple y accesible solo para tu cuenta de Apple. Nosotros no podemos acceder. Los documentos de la bóveda se cifran en tu dispositivo antes de subirse, y la llave se guarda en tu Llavero de iCloud. El espacio cuenta contra tu almacenamiento de iCloud. Puedes desactivarla en Ajustes. Apple trata los datos conforme a su Política de privacidad.

3. Funciones en el dispositivo

El reconocimiento de texto (OCR), la traducción con cámara y de documentos, el reconocimiento de voz, el asistente y "Pregunta a mis documentos" usan los marcos de Apple en el dispositivo (incluidos los Foundation Models de Apple). Tus documentos, fotos, grabaciones y su texto no se envían a nosotros ni a ningún servicio externo de IA o traducción. Si iOS necesita descargar un idioma o modelo, la descarga proviene de Apple.

4. Compartir con la comunidad (opcional, desactivado por defecto)

Puedes elegir, por separado, compartir lo que pagaste (costos), cuánto tardaron los trámites (plazos) y si una estimación fue correcta (resultados). Lo compartido contiene solo rangos redondeados, una zona de la ciudad y un mes o trimestre. Nunca incluye tu nombre, datos de contacto, dirección, documentos ni identificadores de dispositivo o cuenta, y no se vincula contigo. Se envía a la base de datos pública de CloudKit de la app tras un retraso aleatorio, y nada se muestra hasta que al menos 5 personas reporten lo mismo. Puedes retirar tus reportes de costos en Ajustes → Mis aportaciones, y Borrar todos los datos elimina los reportes pendientes. Las estadísticas agregadas que no identifican a nadie pueden conservarse.

5. Reportes, reclamaciones y reseñas del directorio (Rolodex)

Si reportas un problema con un listado, reclamas un listado o respondes la reseña "¿Lo contrataste?" (sí/no), se guarda en la base de datos pública de CloudKit junto con un identificador anónimo de usuario de CloudKit, específico de la app, que proporciona Apple. Lo usamos para evitar abusos y duplicados, permitirte ver o retirar tus envíos, y moderar. Este identificador no nos da tu nombre ni tu correo de Apple. Las reseñas se muestran solo en conjunto; los reportes solo los leen los moderadores.

6. Compras

Apple procesa las compras y suscripciones dentro de la app. No recibimos los datos de tu tarjeta. La app verifica tus compras en el dispositivo, y podemos recibir de Apple el estado de compras y suscripciones (no vinculado a tu identidad) para darte lo que compraste y soporte. La mayor parte de la app es gratis y no requiere compra ni cuenta; comprar Plus o Pro no nos envía más información sobre ti. Los enlaces y códigos QR del App Store que compartimos (incluidos los que muestran los negocios) pueden llevar el nombre de una campaña para que Apple nos muestre el total de descargas por campaña. Nunca identifican quién compartió, escaneó o descargó.

7. Portal para negocios (solo negocios)

Los negocios que administran un listado inician sesión con Iniciar sesión con Apple. Tratamos los datos de contacto del negocio que proporcionas (nombre del negocio, nombre de contacto, teléfono, correo, sitio web, dirección, categoría, zonas de servicio, contenido del perfil, y el logo y las fotos que subas) para publicar y administrar el listado, verificar reclamaciones y prestar las funciones Pro. Los pagos Pro en la web los procesa Stripe, que recibe tus datos de pago conforme a su Política de privacidad; nosotros recibimos solo el estado de facturación y datos limitados (como los últimos cuatro dígitos y la vigencia). Nuestro equipo revisa las fotos y logos antes de mostrarlos; solo se muestran las imágenes aprobadas y puedes eliminarlas en cualquier momento. La información aprobada del listado, incluida tu página pública (por ejemplo /p/tu-listado), la insignia de verificado y el código QR, es pública por diseño.

7a. Conteo anónimo de vistas de listados

Cuando alguien abre la página pública de un negocio o su ficha en la app, sumamos uno al total de vistas de ese día. Solo guardamos el listado, la fecha (solo el día) y el número. No guardamos quién lo vio, direcciones IP, identificadores de dispositivo o cuenta, cookies, la hora ni de dónde llegó, y los conteos no pueden vincularse con nadie. La app envía como máximo un conteo por listado al día y solo incluye el identificador del listado. Los negocios solo ven totales.

7b. Día de la firma, reuniones, documentos compartidos, insignias y reseñas

  • Seguimiento del día de la firma (Actividad en vivo). Si lo inicias, tu pantalla bloqueada muestra en qué punto de la firma vas. Solo contiene el número del punto y un cronómetro. Nunca incluye nombres, montos ni direcciones, y no se nos envía nada.
  • Modo reunión bilingüe. La voz se reconoce y traduce en tu iPhone. Las marcas de la agenda existen solo mientras la pantalla está abierta. No se graba, guarda ni envía nada.
  • Revisión de señales de alerta. Tus respuestas se quedan en la memoria de tu iPhone y se borran al cerrar la pantalla. Nunca se guardan ni se envían.
  • Compartir documentos con un heredero o copropietario (apagado por defecto). Solo si lo activas y eliges documentos, se guardan copias de esos documentos en tu propia cuenta de iCloud y se comparten, solo para lectura, con las personas que invites desde la pantalla de Apple. Nunca vemos a quién invitas ni los documentos. Esas copias las protege iCloud, no el cifrado adicional de la bóveda. «Dejar de compartir» borra las copias.
  • Sello de verificación e insignia de respuesta. Nuestro equipo revisa registros públicos (SEP, SAT, Colegio de Notarios) y solo guarda el resultado, la fecha y la fuente. Las insignias de respuesta se basan en tiempos de respuesta que confirman los negocios, al menos 5 en 90 días, sin identidad del comprador.
  • Rangos de costos de la comunidad y tiempos de «compradores como tú». Se muestran solo cuando al menos 5 personas aportaron, como rangos redondeados. No se puede recuperar ninguna aportación individual. Son estimaciones.
  • Reseñas escritas. Texto opcional (hasta 500 caracteres). Quitamos teléfonos, correos e identificaciones en tu dispositivo, y un moderador la revisa antes de publicarla. Las reseñas no se vinculan con tu nombre.
